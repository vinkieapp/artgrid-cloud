ARTGRID prepared synthetic narration

These are pre-generated synthetic character performances, not recordings by a
human narrator and not a live voice or AI service. Only the fixed, public lesson
scripts were synthesized. No microphone, user text, or user audio was used.
Models and synthesis software are not distributed with this website.

English Apollo: Piper en_GB-northern_english_male-medium; OpenSLR83 Northern
English speech data. Original synthetic character voice, with the approved
brisk delivery increased by 7% and a gentle one-semitone pitch adjustment.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_GB/northern_english_male/medium/MODEL_CARD

English Athena: Piper en_GB-southern_english_female-low; OpenSLR83 Southern
English speech data. Original synthetic character voice at the approved pace.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_GB/southern_english_female/low/MODEL_CARD
