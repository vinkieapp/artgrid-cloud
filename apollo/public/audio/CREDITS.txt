ARTGRID prepared synthetic narration

These are pre-generated synthetic character performances, not recordings by a
human narrator and not a live voice or AI service. Only the fixed, public lesson
scripts were synthesized. No microphone, user text, or user audio was used.
Models and synthesis software are not distributed with this website.

English Apollo: Piper en_US-john-medium; trained on public-domain LibriVox
recordings, assembled by Bryce Beattie.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/john/medium/MODEL_CARD
https://librivox.org

English Athena: Piper en_US-ljspeech-medium; LJ Speech public-domain dataset
compiled by Keith Ito and Linda Johnson; model by Bryce Beattie.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium/MODEL_CARD
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/

French Apollo: Piper fr_FR-upmc-medium, Pierre speaker; UPMC Pierre voice data,
Pierre Chauvin, ISIR/Université Pierre et Marie Curie and MaryTTS contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/fr/fr_FR/upmc/medium/MODEL_CARD
https://github.com/marytts/upmc-pierre-data
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

French Athena: Piper fr_FR-siwis-medium; SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database,
Junichi Yamagishi, Pierre-Edouard Honnet, Philip Garner and Alexandros Lazaridis (2017), University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/fr/fr_FR/siwis/medium/MODEL_CARD
https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: new synthesized ARTGRID scripts, normalized mono PCM, concatenation
